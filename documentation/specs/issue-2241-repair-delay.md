# Issue #2241 : délai repair / Repair Delay

Version 0.3 ; 2026-11-15 ; architecture centralisée approuvée, autorisation documentaire seulement, attente d'autorisation de développement après nouvelle validation utilisateur / centralized architecture approved, documentation only authorized, awaiting development authorization after renewed user validation.

## Français : spécification et conception
### Périmètre
Réduire les faux repairs dus au retour d'état retardé. Existant analysé : aucun délai repair ni horodatage des ON/OFF directs pour les switches ; délai de 2 s pour climates/valves. L'utilisateur active l'option repair ; VTherm contrôle les sous-jacents ; HA reçoit les commandes et expose leur état, sans garantie d'application physique.
### Spécification
- **FR-001** Le gestionnaire doit différer le repair de 10 s après le dernier envoi de chaque objet sous-jacent : switch, climate, valve et valve regulation.
- **FR-002** Le gestionnaire doit sauter l'appel au check du sous-jacent avant 10 s, sans commande, warning ni tentative attributable à ce skip ; reprendre au prochain contrôle existant, sans timer repair ni SLA. Le check direct du sous-jacent ne porte pas cette garde temporelle.
- **FR-003** Le système doit renouveler l'horodatage local après tout appel de service accepté sans échec signalé, même identique : ON/OFF, keep-alive, repair, température, ventilation, humidité, swing et autres commandes. Une programmation ou un non-envoi ne doit pas le renouveler.
- **FR-004** Le système doit garder les comparaisons existantes : ON/OFF logique switch, `hvac_mode` attendu climate, ouverture attendue valve. Une différence de consigne seule ne doit pas déclencher de repair.
- **FR-005** Le système doit garder #1900 : renvoi inconditionnel de consigne à 2 s après HEAT/COOL, y compris après repair et si la consigne est conforme, indépendant de l'option/éligibilité repair ; OFF annule le renvoi en attente. Le délai ventilation reste à 2 s.
- **BR-001** Le système doit ignorer un âge `< 10 s` ; à `>= 10 s`, évaluer l'écart au prochain check, sous réserve des protections existantes, sans garantie d'exécution à l'échéance.
- **BR-002** Le système doit repartir de chaque envoi accepté : des commandes/keep-alive espacés de moins de 10 s peuvent différer le repair indéfiniment. Cette limite est acceptée.
- **BR-003** Le système doit conserver inversion/commandes personnalisées, correspondances de modes dont SLEEP, tolérance d'ouverture `<= 0,5` sans repair, seuils et corrections. La fermeture OFF reste réparable sans restaurer une ancienne ouverture ; aucune comparaison séparée de fermeture n'est ajoutée.
- **BR-004** Le système doit isoler les horodatages par objet. La paire ouverture/fermeture partage celui de sa valve regulation ; chaque succès le renouvelle, un échec ultérieur ne l'efface pas. Le climate associé reste indépendant.
- **BR-005** Le système doit conserver initialisation, pauses et comptage global : un skip n'est pas une tentative, mais ne garantit pas le gel du compteur ni l'absence d'incrément pour un autre repair.

### Conception
- **D-01** Prévoir `REPAIR_COMMAND_GRACE_SEC = 10` dans `const.py`, importée dans `feature_repair_incorrect_state_manager.py`, dédiée au repair ; exposer sur `UnderlyingEntity` la property en lecture seule `last_command_sent_datetime -> datetime`, renvoyant `_last_command_sent_datetime`. Garder `resend_delay_sec = 2` et ses usages #1900/ventilation.
- **D-02** Dans `FeatureRepairIncorrectStateManager.check_and_repair`, avant CHAQUE `await underlying.check_and_repair()`, lire `self._vtherm.now` et `underlying.last_command_sent_datetime` ; si `now < last_sent + timedelta(seconds=REPAIR_COMMAND_GRACE_SEC)`, faire `continue` sans appeler le check. À `>= 10 s`, appeler le check existant, sous réserve des protections globales ; aucun warning/incrément attributable au skip.
- **D-03** Prévoir `_last_command_sent_datetime = self._thermostat.now` immédiatement après chacun des appels directs switch ON/OFF réussis, avant mise à jour du cycle/réarmement keep-alive. Garder paramètres, ordre des opérations de puissance, retours et exceptions : annulation keep-alive, `ServiceNotFound` journalisée, autres erreurs propagées. Ne pas remplacer ces appels par le wrapper ni ajouter d'abstraction.
- Retirer les gardes temporelles actuelles des checks climate/valve ; ne pas en ajouter au switch. Valve regulation hérite du check valve. Ces checks directs conservent uniquement leurs comparaisons/corrections spécifiques et protections existantes, dont OFF ; aucune protection temporelle repair ni comparaison de consigne.
- Pour climate/valves, conserver `hass_services_async_call`/`send_value_to_number` et leurs erreurs absorbées. Dater au point d'envoi, pas selon une réponse HA (éventuellement `None`) ou un booléen de haut niveau. Aucun horodatage pour échec, blocage (inhibition/puissance), déduplication, absence d'envoi, cache/état attendu ou callback programmé ; une erreur de hook/réarmement après succès n'efface pas l'envoi.
- Paire : ouverture puis fermeture optionnelle, chaque service réussi date le même objet ; succès/échec conserve la première date, échec/succès retient la seconde, deux échecs ne changent rien. Garder caches/hooks/poursuite existants, sans transaction ni rollback ; aucun horodatage partagé avec le climate.
- #1900 conserve sélection dernière consigne envoyée, puis régulée, puis cible, protections initialisation/inhibition et absence d'envoi sans valeur. Repair HEAT/COOL à t puis renvoi accepté à t+2 s : prochaine éligibilité au plus tôt t+12 s ; callback retardé : repartir de l'envoi réel. OFF conserve l'annulation. Ventilation : garder `now > last_sent + 2 s` et reprogrammation, sans promesse d'envoi exactement à 2 s.
- Réutiliser objets/`entity_id` et époque UTC initiale, sans persistance/migration ni grâce depuis le redémarrage. Garde sur `_vtherm.now`, cohérente avec les dates `_thermostat.now` ; horloge UTC existante du ready-delay non refactorée. Point d'appel inchangé ; gestionnaire seul responsable du filtrage (debug permis). Hors pause, au moins un `True` incrémente une fois, sinon reset global avec seuls skips ; pauses, limite 5 tentatives, délai initial 30 s et retours des checks inchangés.

## English: Specification and Design
### Scope
Reduce false repairs caused by delayed state feedback. Analyzed baseline: switches have no repair delay or direct ON/OFF timestamping; climates/valves have a 2 s delay. The user enables repair; VTherm checks underlyings; HA receives commands and exposes their state, without guaranteeing physical application.
### Specification
- **FR-001** The manager shall defer repair for 10 s after each underlying object's last send: switch, climate, valve and valve regulation.
- **FR-002** The manager shall skip calling the underlying check before 10 s, without a command, warning or attempt attributable to this skip; resume at the next existing control, without a repair timer or SLA. The underlying's direct check shall not carry this temporal guard.
- **FR-003** The system shall renew the local timestamp after every service call accepted without a reported failure, even if identical: ON/OFF, keep-alive, repair, temperature, fan, humidity, swing and other commands. Scheduling or not sending shall not renew it.
- **FR-004** The system shall retain existing comparisons: logical switch ON/OFF, expected climate `hvac_mode`, expected valve opening. A setpoint-only mismatch shall not trigger repair.
- **FR-005** The system shall retain #1900: unconditional setpoint resend at 2 s after HEAT/COOL, including after repair and when the setpoint matches, independent of repair option/eligibility; OFF cancels pending resend. Fan delay remains 2 s.
- **BR-001** The system shall skip an age `< 10 s`; at `>= 10 s`, evaluate the mismatch at the next check, subject to existing protections, without guaranteeing execution at expiry.
- **BR-002** The system shall restart the age after every accepted send: commands/keep-alive less than 10 s apart can defer repair indefinitely. This limitation is accepted.
- **BR-003** The system shall retain inversion/custom commands, mode mappings including SLEEP, opening tolerance `<= 0.5` without repair, thresholds and corrections. OFF closure remains repairable without restoring a stale opening; no separate closing comparison is added.
- **BR-004** The system shall isolate timestamps per object. The opening/closing pair shares its valve-regulation timestamp; every success renews it, a later failure does not erase it. The associated climate remains independent.
- **BR-005** The system shall preserve initialization, pauses and global accounting: a skip is not an attempt, but guarantees neither a frozen counter nor no increment for another repair.

### Design
- **D-01** Plan `REPAIR_COMMAND_GRACE_SEC = 10` in `const.py`, imported into `feature_repair_incorrect_state_manager.py`, dedicated to repair; expose the read-only property `last_command_sent_datetime -> datetime` on `UnderlyingEntity`, returning `_last_command_sent_datetime`. Retain `resend_delay_sec = 2` and its #1900/fan uses.
- **D-02** In `FeatureRepairIncorrectStateManager.check_and_repair`, before EVERY `await underlying.check_and_repair()`, read `self._vtherm.now` and `underlying.last_command_sent_datetime`; if `now < last_sent + timedelta(seconds=REPAIR_COMMAND_GRACE_SEC)`, use `continue` without calling the check. At `>= 10 s`, call the existing check, subject to global protections; no warning/increment attributable to the skip.
- **D-03** Plan `_last_command_sent_datetime = self._thermostat.now` immediately after each successful direct switch ON/OFF call, before cycle-state update/keep-alive rearming. Retain parameters, power-operation order, returns and exceptions: keep-alive cancellation, logged `ServiceNotFound`, other errors propagated. Do not replace these calls with the wrapper or add an abstraction.
- Remove current temporal guards from climate/valve checks; add none to the switch. Valve regulation inherits the valve check. Direct checks retain only their type-specific comparisons/corrections and existing protections, including OFF; no temporal repair protection or setpoint comparison.
- For climate/valves, retain `hass_services_async_call`/`send_value_to_number` and their absorbed errors. Timestamp at the send point, not according to an HA response (possibly `None`) or a higher-level boolean. No timestamp for failure, blocking (inhibition/power), deduplication, no send, cache/expected-state change or scheduled callback; a hook/rearming error after success does not erase the send.
- Pair: opening then optional closing, each successful service timestamps the same object; success/failure retains the first timestamp, failure/success retains the second, two failures change nothing. Retain existing caches/hooks/continuation, without transaction or rollback; no timestamp shared with the climate.
- #1900 retains selection of last sent setpoint, then regulated target, then target, initialization/inhibition protections and no send without a value. HEAT/COOL repair at t then accepted resend at t+2 s: next eligibility no earlier than t+12 s; delayed callback: use the actual send time. OFF retains cancellation. Fan: retain `now > last_sent + 2 s` and rescheduling, without promising a send exactly at 2 s.
- Reuse objects/`entity_id` and initial UTC epoch, without persistence/migration or grace from restart. Guard uses `_vtherm.now`, consistent with `_thermostat.now` timestamps; existing UTC ready-delay clock is not refactored. Call site unchanged; manager alone owns filtering (debug allowed). Outside pauses, at least one `True` increments once, otherwise skips alone reset the global counter; pauses, 5-attempt limit, 30 s initial delay and check returns remain unchanged.

### Expected Tests
- **AC-001** Through the manager, four types, persistent mismatch: at 0/9.999 s check not called, no attributable repair/warning/attempt; at 10/10.001 s check called and correction eligible, other protections cleared.
- **AC-002** Matching state: no repair; matching climate mode but setpoint-only mismatch after 10 s: no repair.
- **AC-003** Skip then next control after 10 s without another send: mismatch repairable, no repair callback/SLA. Direct calls to all four types' checks below 10 s: no temporal guard, specific corrections/protections retained; migrate old 2 s guard tests to the manager.
- **AC-004** Manager with several types/distinct dates, including pair and associated climate: independent eligibility times after another send; mocks expose public property `last_command_sent_datetime`, read before each check.
- **AC-005** Successful direct ON/OFF, inverted/custom commands, repair and other services: local timestamp renewed; repeated keep-alive below 10 s maintains ineligibility, without changing cancellation/rearming/backoff.
- **AC-006** Inhibition, power, deduplication/no send, merely scheduled callback, `ServiceNotFound`/other error without a successful send: unchanged timestamp and preserved contracts; error after success: retain that send's timestamp; cover all paired success/failure combinations.
- **AC-007** Direct/regulation valves with/without closing: opening, tolerance, thresholds, OFF closure without stale demand; no new comparison.
- **AC-008** HEAT and COOL repairs then #1900 at 2 s, even with matching setpoint: selection/protections intact, timestamp renewed, skip before t+12 s; prior OFF cancels without waking the climate.
- **AC-009** #1900 independent of repair and fan: retain 2 s delays, fan immediate only after more than 2 s otherwise delayed/rescheduled; timestamp on send, no repair timer.
- **AC-010** Disabled option, initialization, inhibition, HVAC OFF, missing/unknown/unavailable states, thresholds, errors/APIs, pauses/counters: skips alone reset outside pauses; skip+repair increments once; the manager logs a repair warning only when the check reports a repair, other error logs unchanged.
Adapt existing tests after renewed user validation authorizing development, without real waits: consistent `_vtherm.now`/`_thermostat.now` and separate UTC ready-delay clock, protections cleared per scenario; mock the HA service without replacing the send/wrapper to prove timestamping. Migrate 2/3 s repair boundaries to manager tests, add direct checks without guards; leave #1900 unchanged. Wrapper mocks: selection/scheduling only. No application tests executed, no implementation.

### Limits and Follow-up
Excluded: temperature repair, feedback-conditional #1900, timers/deadlines, configurable delay, regulation, counter redesign, extra comparisons, general error fixes. Normal/protection/OFF commands, APIs, configuration, dependencies and environment remain unchanged; no new service/permission/exposed state/private data or established regulatory constraint.
Assumption: successful call without reported failure, not hardware acknowledgment. Accepted risks: cloud feedback beyond 10 s, indefinite deferrals, dependency on checks/protections and clock adjustments (no monotonic guarantee). Same functional scope and acceptance coverage; approved variant is feasible/testable, no identified blocker. Separate hardware trace; sources: report and targeted static reading, referenced tests not rerun; no HA inspection, demonstrated hardware behavior or introducing commit.
