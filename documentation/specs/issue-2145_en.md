## 2. Context and objectives

The report describes brief heating interruptions at the start of cycles of an `over_switch` with three inverted relays. The local path permits double counting: at 100% duty, the heating request is renewed for each relay while the multi-relay power check evaluates positive startup power even when the relay's HA state already indicates heating. The objective is to avoid denial and the `power` preset caused solely by that repetition, while preserving checks for relays not recognized as heating.

Accepted scope: per-individual-relay marginal decision for `turn_on` on a multi-relay `over_switch`, notably at 100% cycle restart, including inverted relays; single-relay behavior and checks/reservations for other relays are preserved. A relay already reported as heating by Home Assistant is exempt from the new marginal count. If its consumption is not yet included in the power measurement, the configured threshold may be temporarily exceeded. HA state proves neither physical contact nor measurement inclusion, and this software management does not protect the circuit breaker.

## 3. Actors and use cases

| Actor                    | Role                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| User / installer         | Configures relays, command direction, total power and power sensors; observes denials and the preset.        |
| Multi-relay thermostat   | Renews the heating request at the start of a 100% cycle.                                                     |
| Power manager            | Evaluates headroom and possible starts, then retains reservations until the next measurement event.          |
| Home Assistant / devices | Supply states and measurements that may be absent or delayed; reported state is not proof of physical state. |

**UC-01 — Continue reported heating:** a 100% cycle restarts and each relay's current known HA state indicates heating in its configured direction. Reissuing these commands shall not create new marginal power demand or trigger the `power` preset for that reason alone; measurement inclusion is not assumed proven, and the under-reservation risk described in BR-005 is accepted.

**UC-02 — Start a distinct relay:** one relay heats and another is stopped; the second relay's heating request shall be evaluated as a new start regardless of overall thermostat activity. If headroom is insufficient, existing denial and shedding behavior applies.

**UC-03 — State not recognized as heating:** a relay is stopped according to HA, `unknown`, `unavailable` or absent; its request retains the existing conservative check and reservation. Missing measurement remains subject to current behavior, with no guarantee of an effective check.

## 6. Functional constraints

- **Compatibility:** retain existing settings, exposed states and threshold rules; no migration is planned in this scope.
- **Safety and availability:** do not exempt an HA state not recognized as heating; exemption for a recognized HA state can still under-reserve (BR-005). Software checks do not guarantee circuit-breaker protection, and missing measurement or threshold retains existing permissive behavior.
- **Units:** the reported 5,600–5,700 VA and declared 5,000 W are not physically interchangeable without a power-factor assumption; numerical examples only reproduce the software behavior described in the report.
- **Privacy:** site traces used for validation shall be stripped of identifiers and personal data.

## 8. Out-of-scope functions

- No blanket bypass of checks at cycle start or change to keep-alive commands: this could mask a genuinely new start.
- No change to global priority shedding, TPI, thresholds, external automations or a real Home Assistant installation: these are distinct paths or outside the reported problem.
- No declared per-relay power model or regulatory VA/W conversion: additional decisions and data would be required.
- No changes to measurement freshness, sensor-event reservation clearing policy, or a Linky inclusion proof contract: separate topics, unnecessary for the adopted local criterion.
- No retroactive correction of past logs or measurements: their synchronization was not verified directly.

## 9. Proposed future evolution

- Investigate individually configured relay power if the need to cover heterogeneous loads is confirmed; decide on configuration model and compatibility before requiring it.
- If the BR-005 risk is rejected, separately reconsider means of obtaining sufficient assurance without assuming any are available today.

## 10. Assumptions, open questions and traceability

**Locally verified facts:** before the fix, `CycleScheduler._start_cycle_switch` called `turn_on()` for every relay at 100%, and the multi-relay calculation assigned `device_power / nb_underlying_entities` even to active relays. The current implementation passes the reservation key to the shared calculator, which finds the matching underlying and reads its `is_device_active`: an active relay has zero marginal startup power, while a stopped relay or one with an uncertain state retains its startup share. The shared strict formula still applies; adding zero does not erase a pending reservation. `FeatureCentralPowerManager._power_sensor_changed` clears reservations on an event, including identical-value events; `test_power_feature_manager_reserves_startup_power_per_underlying_for_multi_underlyings` checks distinct keys. None of these facts proves Linky inclusion. See linked metadata sources.

**Unverified site assumptions:** the reported cycle was at 100%, HA states reflected physical contacts and the Linky measurement included the loads. The implemented exemption accepts that a current HA state may be stale and that the measurement may not include the load; it does not assume these facts are proven. The review summarizes issue comments, but the raw attached log was not verified.

**Change since v1.0:** FR-002/BR-002/AC-001/AC-011 no longer require causal proof of inclusion unavailable locally; FR-006/AC-008 fix conservative handling of states not recognized as heating. BR-006/AC-007/AC-009 record existing sensor behavior without changing it.

**Decision and authorization, 2026-10-26:** the owner accepted BR-005 option A and its under-reservation risk; development is authorized within the scope defined here. A relay with a known HA heating state is exempt from marginal counting even if its load is not yet visible in the measurement, which can temporarily exceed the configured threshold. No Linky proof contract, new parameter or sensor-event handling change is introduced.
