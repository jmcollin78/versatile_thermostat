## 2. Context and objectives

The report describes brief heating interruptions at the start of cycles of an `over_switch` with three inverted relays. The local path permits double counting: at 100% duty, the heating request is renewed for each relay while the multi-relay power check evaluates positive startup power even when the relay's HA state already indicates heating. The proposed objective is to avoid denial and the `power` preset caused solely by that repetition, while preserving checks for relays not recognized as heating.

Proposed current scope: per-individual-relay marginal decision for `turn_on` on a multi-relay `over_switch`, notably at 100% cycle restart, including inverted relays; preservation of single-relay behavior and existing checks/reservations for other relays. No local path proves that the Linky has incorporated a load: known HA state indicates heating, not physical contact or inclusion in a measurement. This conditional exemption can under-reserve power; it is neither proof of the diagnosis at the reporter's site nor a guarantee of electrical protection.

## 3. Actors and use cases

| Actor                    | Role                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| User / installer         | Configures relays, command direction, total power and power sensors; observes denials and the preset.        |
| Multi-relay thermostat   | Renews the heating request at the start of a 100% cycle.                                                     |
| Power manager            | Evaluates headroom and possible starts, then retains reservations until the next measurement event.          |
| Home Assistant / devices | Supply states and measurements that may be absent or delayed; reported state is not proof of physical state. |

**UC-01 — Continue reported heating:** a 100% cycle restarts and each relay's current known HA state indicates heating in its configured direction. Subject to business acceptance of the risk, reissuing these commands shall not create a new power demand or trigger the `power` preset for that reason alone; inclusion in the measurement is not assumed proven.

**UC-02 — Start a distinct relay:** one relay heats and another is stopped; the second relay's heating request shall be evaluated as a new start regardless of overall thermostat activity. If headroom is insufficient, existing denial and shedding behavior applies.

**UC-03 — State not recognized as heating:** a relay is stopped according to HA, `unknown`, `unavailable` or absent; its request retains the existing conservative check and reservation. Missing measurement remains subject to current behavior, with no guarantee of an effective check.

## 4. Functional requirements

| ID     | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FR-001 | The system shall evaluate each `turn_on` request per individual relay without inferring its status from overall thermostat state alone.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| FR-002 | When an individual relay's current HA state is known and matches its configured heating state, the system shall assign zero marginal startup power to its `turn_on`, retain the command and not deny this repetition alone for overload. Inclusion in the measurement is neither a prerequisite nor a proven consequence. |
| FR-003 | The system shall check headroom for another relay not recognized as heating and reserve its own startup power according to existing behavior; denial shall retain existing shedding behavior.                                                                                                                             |
| FR-004 | The system shall interpret heating state according to each relay's configured command direction, including when `off` means heating.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| FR-005 | The system shall retain existing single-relay power-check behavior; changing it is not required by this multi-relay correction.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| FR-006 | The system shall retain existing conservative checks and reservations for an HA state that is stopped, `unknown`, `unavailable` or absent, without treating a `False` boolean alone as proof of a known stopped state.                                                                                                    |
| FR-007 | The system shall not present HA state or a power-sensor event, even one reporting an identical value, as proof that the measurement includes the load.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| FR-008 | The system shall retain existing reservations for the same key and their accumulation across distinct relays until the current sensor-event clearing; an exempt repetition shall neither add a reservation nor erase an existing reservation for that relay.                                                              |
| FR-009 | Within this scope, the system shall retain declared total power and the existing startup-power allocation; it shall not present that allocation as a guarantee that physical relay powers are equal.                                                                                                                      |

## 5. Business rules

| ID     | Condition, priority and exception                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

| BR-001 | Per-relay evaluation takes precedence: activity of a first relay never exempts a second stopped relay from its startup check.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| BR-002 | For `turn_on`, a known current HA state matching the individual relay's configured heating state is sufficient to set its marginal startup power to zero; no Linky inclusion is claimed. | BR-003 | A relay not recognized as heating uses declared instantaneous power under the existing model, not cycle duty percentage; with measurement and threshold available, insufficient headroom leads to the usual denial even if another relay is already heating.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| BR-004 | Reservations for distinct relays accumulate; an exempt repetition shall not erase an existing reservation for its own key. Clearing on a sensor event, even with an identical value, remains unchanged and proves no inclusion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| BR-005 | **Blocking business decision before development — stale HA state:** the BR-002 exemption can under-reserve if the physical contact no longer matches HA or if the measurement has not incorporated the load. ## 6. Functional constraints

- **Compatibility:** retain existing settings, exposed states and threshold rules; no migration is planned in this scope.
- **Safety and availability:** do not exempt an HA state not recognized as heating; exemption for a recognized HA state can still under-reserve (BR-005). Software checks do not guarantee circuit-breaker protection, and missing measurement or threshold retains existing permissive behavior.
- **Units:** the reported 5,600–5,700 VA and declared 5,000 W are not physically interchangeable without a power-factor assumption; numerical examples only reproduce the software behavior described in the report.
- **Privacy:** site traces used for validation shall be stripped of identifiers and personal data.

## 8. Out-of-scope functions

- No blanket bypass of checks at cycle start or change to keep-alive commands: this could mask a genuinely new start.
- No change to global priority shedding, TPI, thresholds, external automations or a real Home Assistant installation: these are distinct paths or outside the reported problem.
- No declared per-relay power model or regulatory VA/W conversion: additional decisions and data would be required.
- No changes to measurement freshness, sensor-event reservation clearing policy, or a Linky inclusion proof contract: separate topics, unnecessary for the proposed local criterion.
- No retroactive correction of past logs or measurements: their synchronization was not verified directly.

## 9. Proposed future evolution

- Investigate individually configured relay power if the need to cover heterogeneous loads is confirmed; decide on configuration model and compatibility before requiring it.
- If the BR-005 risk is rejected, separately reconsider means of obtaining sufficient assurance without assuming any are available today.

## 10. Assumptions, open questions and traceability

**Locally verified facts:** `CycleScheduler._start_cycle_switch` calls `turn_on()` for all relays at 100%; `UnderlyingSwitch.turn_on` checks before commanding; `UnderlyingSwitch.is_device_active` compares reported state with the configured heating state, including inversion. After initialization, this comparison may return `False` for `unknown`/`unavailable`: distinguishing the raw state remains to be designed. `FeaturePowerManager.check_power_available` uses a strict threshold and `calculate_underlying_startup_power` currently assigns `device_power / nb_underlying_entities` to multiple relays, even active ones; reservation recalculates the power. `FeatureCentralPowerManager._power_sensor_changed` clears reservations on an event, including identical-value events; `test_power_feature_manager_reserves_startup_power_per_underlying_for_multi_underlyings` checks distinct keys. None of these facts proves Linky inclusion. See linked metadata sources.

**Unverified site assumptions:** the reported cycle was at 100%, HA states reflected physical contacts and the Linky measurement included the loads. The proposed exemption assumes acceptance that a current HA state may be stale and that the measurement may not include the load; it does not assume these facts are proven. The review summarizes issue comments, but the raw attached log was not verified.

**Change since v1.0:** FR-002/BR-002/AC-001/AC-011 no longer require causal proof of inclusion unavailable locally; FR-006/AC-008 fix conservative handling of states not recognized as heating. BR-006/AC-007/AC-009 record existing sensor behavior without changing it.

**Blocking decision before development:** will the business owner accept BR-005 option A and the under-reservation risk from stale HA state or load not yet included in the measurement? Otherwise choose option B without claiming to fix double counting within this scope. No Linky proof architecture, new parameter or sensor-event handling change is requested. Approval of specification and design is not authorization to develop.
