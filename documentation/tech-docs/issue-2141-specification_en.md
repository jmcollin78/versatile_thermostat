## 2. Context and objectives

**#2141 history.** Capture rapid TRV setpoint changes without treating every report as a human action. Established decisions are manual setpoint including while OFF (Q-01), sliding five-second debounce, last command sent per TRV without history, conservative rejection of compatible/nearby reports, and a cap with external rearming (Q-02), plus tracking without a prior send (Q-03). Historical sources describe a broader physical ambition than the coverage currently verified; that difference remains explicit.

**Existing implementation.** #2141 has already been developed across several classes. Its code remains in place and intact, notably in `BaseThermostat`, underlyings, and `CycleScheduler`. It shall be neither moved nor rewritten inside `ThermostatOverClimate`. The restriction below applies only to **new #2159 developments**: it does not retrospectively redefine #2141's scope.

**#2159 target.** Locally fix the historical bypass of HVAC suspension and tracking cause, then preserve the request against a report equal to the effective mode (E-1). Current reading confirms guards and cause in the callback; it does not certify every criterion. Production scope remains limited to **class `ThermostatOverClimate`** in [thermostat_climate.py](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/thermostat_climate.py), reusing #2141's cross-class mechanisms **without modifying other classes or their contracts**. Associated tests may reside elsewhere; this operation modifies only the two specifications.

The candidate baseline qualifies an HVAC transition, preserves the correct source and alignment, respects suspension, and tags adoption. **Decided #2159 change:** locally extend the existing counter to actual mode **and temperature** adoptions, including without downstream sends. It removes no existing attempt debit. One interaction may therefore consume multiple units; common suspension may also block temperature tracking and legitimate actions. This broader logical effect is accepted in the requested need without authorizing a cross-class rewrite. Apart from this shared counting/admission, no new temperature qualification, step, cache, post-clamp deduplication policy, timed-manager redesign, E1/E2 system, or universal propagation is added. Q-07/Q-08/Q-09 remain out of scope.

## 4. Functional requirements

Identifiers FR-001 through FR-017 are retained. v3.6 clarifies the E-1 effective-mode guard without reopening Q-05/Q-11/Q-12 or the shared counter; removed v3.2 extensions remain removed. H requirements do not declare current-code compliance; C debits are not unchanged historical criteria. C denotes the #2159 origin of a requirement, not a current absence of implementation.

- **FR-001 — Scope (H/C/NR):** The system shall preserve the tracking switch and direct control. With tracking disabled, no new tracked adoption shall occur. Only new #2159 developments shall respect section 2's local boundary; cross-class #2141 code remains intact.
- **FR-002 — Three values (H):** The system shall distinguish VTherm request, last command actually sent to each TRV after clamping, and observation. A successful service shall not be presented as acknowledgement. The discrepancy between that historical reference and the current cache is recorded in D-01 without a normative substitution.
- **FR-003 — Conservative qualification (H):** The system shall compare only with the last command sent to the source TRV, without superseded-command history, and reject reports compatible with or too close to distinguish from it. A step alone shall not prove rounding; a conversion/quantization rule shall be verified before claiming one. No new proximity formula is defined here.
- **FR-004 — Ambiguous report (H):** The system shall preserve request and preset and not resend a correction for an ambiguous difference alone, notably `25.6 → 25.5`. It shall retain observations useful for diagnosis and avoiding unnecessary commands without certifying human or automatic origin.
- **FR-005 — Manual adoption (H/C/NR):** The system shall adopt an admissible setpoint once after stability, update the request, set `NONE`, and cancel the older override without restoration or a new override, including while OFF under BR-002. In addition C, each actual requested-setpoint change thus retained shall consume one shared-counter unit under FR-010/BR-012; a mere pending, cancelled, or request-identical candidate is not such a change. A request-identical candidate admissible against the cache and other guards shall retain its existing manual effects (`NONE`, override cancellation without restoration or a new timer, tagged application), even with zero adoption debit; its tagged `climate` attempts actually reached remain counted.
- **FR-006 — Attribution limit (H):** The system shall conservatively reject undecidable nearby reports, including when necessary capabilities/rules are missing, without promising zero lost actions. Existing distinct-value behavior without a step is discrepancy D-02, not a newly approved policy.
- **FR-007 — Old reports (H):** The system shall document that an old distinct echo may be mistaken for an action without history. Neither that report nor its timer shall rearm the guard. The historical command bound and its actual coverage remain distinguished in D-03.
- **FR-008 — Debounce (H/NR):** The system shall retain a sliding five seconds after registration of the last new candidate value; no intermediate adoption during a burst, duplicates and attribute-only updates without extending it. For a mixed event with an adopted mode, candidate evaluation and possible registration occur after normal return from HVAC application, under the resulting references and guards: the five seconds do not start at initial reception. A refused or unchanged mode leaves the setpoint eligible for evaluation under its own guards, without an adopted-mode application to await. An observation replacing the action shall cancel or requalify the candidate. Neither a global ten-second delay nor HVAC debounce shall be reintroduced.
- **FR-009 — Multiple TRVs (H/C/NR):** The system shall preserve the source TRV for observation, candidate, and its own references, including after iterating over other TRVs or waiting. Waiting shall remain bounded per TRV without a global lock waiting for a slow device; existing candidate arbitration remains preserved. HVAC tracking shall preserve existing alignment of other underlyings with VTherm's effective mode without requiring that they already have the new mode. Historical avoidance of unnecessary sends remains an H expectation; #2159 requires no new post-clamp deduplication.
- **FR-010 — Extended single budget (H/E/C):** The system shall retain the sole #2141 counter shared per VTherm, threshold 3, and its existing rearming. E debits for each tagged `climate` attempt shall be supplemented in C by one unit per requested-mode change and one unit per requested-setpoint change actually adopted from underlying reports, across all sources, even without sends. No second counter, replacement of attempt debits, or adoption/service coalescing is allowed. The third debit, regardless of type, shall immediately trigger existing suspension and its cancellations; subsequent guards refuse. A third adoption debit does not guarantee any subsequent send. Historical Q-02 and this #2159 extension remain distinct; no global physical bound is certified.
- **FR-011 — Lifecycle (H/NR):** The system shall preserve existing candidate cancellations on newer intention, deactivation, source loss, and removal/destruction, and zero budget initialization for a new instance. Stronger unproven guarantees are flagged in D-05 without a new #2159 asynchronous redesign.
- **FR-012 — No prior send (H/C):** The system shall not require a prior VTherm command to track a real admissible change. Historical exclusion of a merely restored temperature without a new change remains an H expectation; it does not become a new general prohibition on temperature tracking at initialization. **HVAC** first-delivery qualification follows FR-013.
- **FR-013 — HVAC transition (C):** On an actually delivered callback, the system shall adopt a mode only if old/new identify the same source TRV, contain different valid modes, and the reported mode differs from both VTherm's effective mode and the request. The system shall refuse adoption of a mode equal to the effective mode even if the request differs, without a mode-adoption debit. Absent/invalid old state or identifiable recovery shall not cause first-mode adoption. A first callback already describing a valid transition distinct from the effective mode shall remain eligible under the other guards without a prior send. Delivered states and comparison with the effective mode are authoritative, not the mere presence or absence of parameter `new_hvac_mode`; a null parameter alone does not prohibit adoption.
- **FR-014 — Adoption cause (C/NR):** For an effective mode adoption, the system shall call `update_states()` with `COMMAND_CAUSE_UNDERLYING_TRACKING`, without an external rearming entry; the existing temperature-adoption cause is retained. The additional adoption debit shall neither erase this cause nor exempt downstream attempts from their existing debit. Mere action/fan handling without adoption shall not receive an artificial HVAC tracking cause. Propagation mechanisms elsewhere remain unchanged.
- **FR-015 — Independent fields (H/C/NR):** The system shall handle action, fan, and setpoint under their own rules. A refused or unchanged mode shall neither suppress their handling nor cancel a candidate for that reason alone. `hvac_action`, fan, or a difference between unchanged TRV mode and VTherm shall not be equated with an HVAC transition. No new temperature policy for initial or wrongly identified pairs is inferred from HVAC refusal. After failed or cancelled mode application, existing behavior does not guarantee subsequent setpoint handling.
- **FR-016 — Diagnosis (H/C):** The system shall make adoption/refusal, source, debit type (mode, temperature, tagged attempt), single-counter value, and suspension distinguishable in local diagnostics without a new cross-cutting mechanism. An adoption debit shall not be described as a proven physical emission. Documentation shall distinguish H/E/C/NR, withdrawn former option O-01, actual limitations, and lack of evidence for loop elimination.
- **FR-017 — Rearming (H/E/NR):** The system shall preserve existing external entries and the rearming hook without creating new ones for #2159. Internal tracking, echoes, timers, regulation, elapsed time, and tracking toggles shall not rearm. Exceptions and concrete entry coverage are distinguished in section 10.

## 5. Business rules

- **BR-001 — Common suspension (C):** The system shall refuse new tracked mode and temperature adoptions when existing protection is suspended or its admission guard disallows it. From the third debit onward, the same suspension applies to tagged commands subsequently encountering the existing guard; no slot is reserved for a send following adoption. Direct control remains subject to its usual protections.
- **BR-002 — Q-01 / OFF (H):** The system shall save a new admissible setpoint after five seconds, request `NONE`, and cancel the override without restoration/a new timer, without lifting forced OFF or switching on manual OFF. It becomes applicable under normal calculation when the cause clears or after explicit switch-on. A new OFF cause invalidates the earlier candidate, not a later admissible action.
- **BR-003 — Last send and proximity (H):** The system shall retain the historical last-command-sent and conservative-rejection decision. The historical scenario of `25.6` sent, compatible `25.5` rejected, new distinct `26.0` eligible as a candidate remains an expectation to verify, not replaced by “difference ≤ one step.” Tensions with existing behavior remain D-01/D-02.
- **BR-004 — Indeterminate origin (H/C):** The system shall apply the same conservative rejection to an action indistinguishable from an echo; neither debounce nor successful service proves origin. For HVAC mode, the system shall refuse a mode equal to VTherm's effective mode as a new request, including for an indistinguishable user action and a different request (FR-013). This refusal shall neither suppress handling of other delivered fields under their own guards nor cancel a candidate for that reason alone.
- **BR-005 — No history (H):** After sends `7` then `21`, only the last is the reference. A late distinct `7` may be wrongly adopted after stability; a `21` report replacing the candidate before its deadline cancels/requalifies it. The budget does not ensure perfect attribution.
- **BR-006 — Unknown capabilities (H):** The system shall invent neither a universal step nor a rounding rule; an undecidable nearby report shall not be automatically adopted. Types, HA keys, units, and numeric boundaries are not redefined in #2159; existing limitations remain D-02.
- **BR-007 — Stability (H):** The system shall recheck the last observation and absence of a newer intention at the five-second deadline before adoption; waiting implies no acknowledgement.
- **BR-008 — Attempt debits retained (H/E/NR):** The system shall preserve every debit before an attempt at existing tagged `climate` points across all destinations. A no-op before that point does not count as an attempt; an attempt debit followed by failure/uncertainty is not refunded. These units remain due in addition to C adoption units under BR-012, without deduplication between categories or a source-TRV exemption. The extended counter therefore measures neither only adoptions nor network emissions with certainty; `number` is not covered.
- **BR-009 — No acknowledgement (H):** The system shall not treat silence, equality, or successful service as proof that older echoes are gone; direct control shall remain possible.
- **BR-010 — HVAC initialization (C):** The system shall refuse first-mode/identifiable-recovery tracking when old is absent, `unknown`, or `unavailable`, or the mode/identity pair is invalid. This rule covers mode, not a new global setpoint filter. Existing direct reconciliation remains independent.
- **BR-011 — Q-03 (H/C):** The system shall permit a real admissible change before VTherm's first send; setpoint after five seconds, valid mode immediately. The callback's first position alone does not justify rejection.
- **BR-012 — Decided adoption debits (C):** The system shall debit the existing counter once for each actual retained requested-mode or requested-setpoint mutation caused by tracking. If both are actually adopted, each shall count; receiving a single event does not merge them. Qualification, pending candidate, rejected/compatible/ambiguous report, request-identical value, and attribute without adoption debit no adoption unit. A misattributed echo actually adopted counts: perfect provenance is not assumed. An adoption abandoned before mutation shall not be counted as completed. Application failure after mutation does not prove absence of adoption; observe request and debits separately without inventing rollback. Local sequencing shall preserve recent intention and cleanup of the affected processing without degrading #2141; P-11 details still require technical justification (AC-026/AC-040), without an implicit new reservation, refund, or universal transaction.
- **BR-013 — Existing continuations (E/C/NR):** The system shall reuse existing cause propagation unchanged, notably the TRVZB resend where it retains the cause. A debited adoption may result in zero, one, or multiple attempts, each still subject to its guard and debit. Adoption followed by an attempt is a real double debit, not an anomaly to deduplicate; multiple destinations may exhaust the budget during one interaction and leave partial alignment. No `number` cap, cycle stop, refund, or universal callback cancellation follows.
- **BR-014 — Mixed event (C/NR):** The system shall preserve the source after the alignment loop and handle mode first, then its application if adopted, then evaluate and possibly register the setpoint candidate under the resulting references and its own guards. HVAC refusal shall not become a global return suppressing other fields. The mode debit and its attempts may suspend temperature tracking; field independence does not bypass this suspension. Actual mode adoption may invalidate an older intention; no unconditional admission of the simultaneous setpoint is guaranteed. If mode application fails or is cancelled, subsequent setpoint handling is not guaranteed: trace phases reached and clean up affected processing, without a new independent-delivery, FIFO, or global-priority guarantee.
- **BR-015 — Q-02 / recovery (H/E):** The system shall preserve rearming on an external command received at existing entries, including an accepted identical value. A new instance starts at zero without being an external command. A rearming automation may defeat the bound across multiple rearmings; that historical limitation is accepted, not fixed here.

## 6. Functional constraints

- **Compatibility:** for #2159, only necessary adjustments within `ThermostatOverClimate` are contemplated; existing #2141 mechanisms in other classes remain in place without modification or relocation. No configuration, architecture, or dependency changes. Calling an existing cross-class contract is not modifying it. Future tests are not restricted to the authorized production file.
- **Priorities:** preserve inhibition, window, safety, central mode, and other existing arbitration; distinguish requested and effective state. No additional HVAC delay, no reinstatement of ten seconds.
- **E-1 user risk and attribution limit:** rejecting a report equal to the effective mode shall preserve the existing request, notably HEAT when the effective mode is OFF. It may also ignore an indistinguishable real action; perfect intention recognition is not guaranteed. Former OFF adoption could lose the HEAT request; actual failure to resume after window closure remains a hypothetical consequence, not tested end-to-end in current sources. Logical request preservation does not prove physical resumption.
- **Availability and local limits:** guards cover events/fields actually delivered. Underlying filters may remove a field or the whole callback; no universal recovery is guaranteed. A restoration resembling a valid transition is not always identifiable.
- **Separate factual operational risk:** an OFF request adopted at 2→3 may become effective/published OFF while B remains HEAT, with the downstream guard preventing transmission on the path considered. Neither recovery nor a shutdown deadline shall be promised; existing repair, disabled by default and conditional, is not a sufficient guarantee. Q-11 adds no threshold-transmission requirement, OFF exemption, obligation to block, or obligation to prove device state. This absence of an additional requirement shall not be presented as general acceptance of a dangerous situation or a new safety decision. AC-039 shall characterize the risk, not certify that it always occurs or has disappeared.
- **Reentrancy and robustness to address locally:** absence of `await` within a local segment shall not serve as proof of a transaction or absence of nested execution. Resuming an expired preset may immediately start an HA task and an untagged application before finalization; AC-040 requires future comparative characterization without concluding a double debit or systematic emission. D-11 is a technical problem, not a new user question: preserving #2141, recent intention, no debit for a cancelled candidate without adoption, and local cleanup sufficiently frame its handling. A demonstrated impossibility within the local scope or an unresolved blocking risk shall be reported precisely, not silently approved.
- **Actual protection and usage cost:** in the former code described as E by v3.5, zero tagged attempts implied zero debit; under C and the current code read, an actual adoption consumes even without an attempt. A mixture of mode/temperature actions and sends may exhaust the shared budget, even during one multi-TRV interaction. Suspension also stops temperature tracking and cancels still-registered candidates; legitimate actions, even spaced apart, may be blocked until existing rearming. No time-based or field-specific rearming is added. Fewer than three adoptions may be available; these are not two budgets of three.
- **Outside local control:** `number` services, cycles, direct reconciliations, already-armed callbacks/already-launched commands, and external automations are not universally stopped. An old tagged callback may consume a rearmed budget; no new continuation system is requested.
- **Privacy:** no external service or new personal data required; no additional regulatory constraint established. Diagnostics shall not claim to prove the actor or origin of the first `off`.

Also check the former override deadline.
- **AC-012 — Lifecycle (H/NR):** With an already-registered candidate, deactivation, source removal, unavailability, and destruction prevent adoption at its old deadline; new instance, budget zero. Exercise existing hooks and flag any pre-existing gap; no universal cancellation required.
- **AC-013 — Historical attempt budget (H/E/C/NR):** Without injecting adoption, produce three debits at actual tagged `climate` points, third attempt admitted then suspension, fourth refused; failure after debit without refund. Then deliver an HVAC transition: no adoption or adoption debit despite active tracking. Also test true suspension with counter below three and guard refusing counter ≥ 3; direct control subject to usual guards. These isolated historical attempt tests remain distinct from integrated C scenarios where adoptions also consume.
- **AC-014 — Q-03 (H/NR):** Without a send reference, new observable admissible temperature change: candidate then adoption at five seconds. Mere restoration without a change remains the historical exclusion to check, without a new prohibition based solely on absent/invalid old state.
- **AC-015 — Mixed fields (C/NR):** Mode refused for alignment/suspension or without a transition, with setpoint and action/fan: independent fields handled, no mode-adoption unit or cancellation due to its refusal alone. Setpoint retains its own guards, including common suspension. Adopted mode: older intention invalidated; trace mode/application then setpoint evaluation and possible registration with the resulting references. If the setpoint is also actually adopted after stability from that registration, it consumes its own unit in addition to the mode and attempts. If suspension caused by the mode or attempts prevents it, no temperature-adoption debit. Test at counters 0/1/2: normal success, a guard becoming refusing, failed application, and cancelled application. The last two cases do not guarantee subsequent setpoint handling; record phases reached, absence of a fictitious candidate/adoption, and local cleanup without inventing independent delivery.

### 10.4 Static local feasibility, threshold consequences, and limits

**Provenance:** retained v3.5 analysis, including its then-future stages and validation blockers; this is not the current development status. Section 10.6 is authoritative for the callback after the E-1 fix and current tests. Threshold, OFF, shared-counter, and scope limitations remain unchanged.

- **Available local points:** mode reception and temperature debounce completion are both within `ThermostatOverClimate` ([callback](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/thermostat_climate.py#L751-L944)). Counter, guard, and rearming are local too ([hooks](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/thermostat_climate.py#L974-L1013)). Setters distinguish values without requiring contract changes ([requested state](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/vtherm_state.py#L59-L90)). This makes a local counting extension plausible without a second counter or common-class changes; it is not a validated sequencing design.
- **Third debit:** in E, `_record_underlying_tracking_command()` immediately suspends and invokes candidate cancellation. If that debit is an already-admitted attempt, the service path proceeds without a second guard there. If C produces it for adoption, tagged commands subsequently encountering `_can_send_underlying_tracking_command()` are refused: there is no “third adoption with guaranteed send.” An already-admitted/launched command remains a separate case, not universally cancelled.
- **Candidate still present or already removed:** according to prior analysis, cancellation only iterates registered candidates. The E temperature callback removes its own then the others before mutating the request; a debit after that removal cannot retroactively cancel that candidate through the collection. A debit while it remains registered can cancel it: this does not permit counting an unrealized adoption. Local details still require justification under BR-012 and AC-026/AC-040, not user approval as a universal transaction.
- **Unchanged cross-class guard/service:** [application](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/base_thermostat.py#L1781-L1855) already propagates the cause; [HVAC](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/underlyings.py#L644-L702) and [temperature](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/underlyings.py#L941-L994) guard and debit their attempts. Their signatures, protections, and debit points shall not be rewritten. A different integrated total is a decided C consequence, not authorization to remove those debits or failure of the isolated historical test.
- **Broader logical effect without broader code scope:** the [valve variant](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/thermostat_climate_valve.py#L457-L471) inherits the callback outside recalibration. Its reception can therefore experience common suspension without modifying its class. Its [number services](../../custom_components/versatile_thermostat/underlyings.py#L265-L283), recalculations, and cycles are not universally bounded. Any solution requiring their rewrite would exceed the authorized scope.
- **Saturating OFF and repair:** for an adoption debited before the downstream guard, counter 2, A HEAT→OFF, and B HEAT may lead to requested/effective/published OFF without OFF transmission to B after debit 3. Publication does not prove device shutdown and D-10 guarantees no recovery. Q-11 imposes no additional OFF requirement and is no longer a blocking user decision on that basis; AC-039 retains risk characterization without general acceptance of danger or a new safety decision.
- **Expiration reentrancy:** prior observation D-11 describes expired-timer resumption potentially starting preset code immediately through HA 2026.8.2, reaching an untagged application before finalization. Absence of `await` proves no atomicity; adjacent mutation/debit prove no transaction. Neither double debit nor systematic service is demonstrated. AC-040 shall characterize and address non-regression, recent intention, actual debits, and cleanup locally without changing the manager or HA. This is not a new user question if these requirements suffice.
- **Assessment and precise blockers:** Q-05/Q-11/Q-12 decisions recorded within their functional scope, but final human validation and convergence remain pending. D-11 remains a technical point unresolved in this documentary pass; no new static local impossibility is established here and no test result proves its resolution. If future analysis demonstrates inability to meet requirements within `ThermostatOverClimate` or an unresolved blocking risk, it shall identify the path, requirement, and missing elements without silently broadening scope.
