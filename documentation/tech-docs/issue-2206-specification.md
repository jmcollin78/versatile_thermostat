### 2. Contexte et objectifs

Un refus est normal lorsque la puissance mesuree de 7 600 et le demarrage de 3 500 depassent le seuil de 11 000. Le probleme a traiter est la reaction au refus : elle peut redemarrer immediatement le controle qui vient de demander ON, multiplier les cycles et laisser plusieurs rappels de fin de cycle. Les rafales peuvent alors amplifier le compteur d'energie et les notifications, sans preuve de chauffe physique incorrecte.

**Faits relus dans le code local :**

- `UnderlyingSwitch.turn_on()` force une mise a jour d'etat apres chaque refus, meme si le delestage etait deja actif (S-01).
- La premiere transition de delestage annule le cycle et demande OFF a tous les sous-jacents ; le preset `power` reduit la consigne mais ne signifie pas HVAC OFF (S-02/S-03).
- Les indicateurs de changement d'etat restent vrais jusqu'au retour du controle de chauffe ; les setters conservent un changement deja vrai (S-03).
- Le controle TPI peut rappeler `start_cycle(force=True)` ; la garde de cycle deja actif ne retient que le chemin non force. Les timers sont installes apres l'attente de `turn_on()` (S-04/S-05).
- Une fin de cycle appelle `incremente_energy()` puis relance le cycle ; la formule utilise le taux brut de l'algorithme (S-05/S-06).

**Hypothese locale falsifiable H-01 :** le recalcul force apres refus entretient une reentrance inutile ; des appels imbriques reprenant apres les annulations peuvent installer plusieurs timers dont certaines references sont perdues. La lecture soutient ce mecanisme, mais ni sa reproduction ni le nombre de timers survivants ne sont acquis. Un seul redemarrage imbrique suffit potentiellement : supprimer seulement les repetitions apres le premier refus n'est pas une preuve de correction.

**Objectif P0 :** traiter ce declencheur au plus pres du refus : propager immediatement l'etat sans redemarrer positivement la chauffe depuis ce refus, puis abandonner la tentative refusee avant toute commande ON suivante ou tout rearmement. Conserver premier OFF, reconciliation, idempotence stable et reprise legitime. Le nettoyage deja deleste doit aussi demander OFF aux relais du cycle encore courant : supprimer les timers seuls laisserait potentiellement un relais allume sans arret programme.

**Perimetre A propose :** chemin natif TPI `over_switch`, mono/multi, virtuel/inverse ; premier refus reel et refus suivants ; etat coherent ou a reconcilier ; OFF effectif rencontre pendant les attentes d'une tentative qui rend effectivement un refus ; reprises et changements positifs serialises. Partir d'au plus un jeu de timers reference, sans orphelins anterieurs ; mesurer apres terminaison des traitements bornes. Zero timer scheduler apres abandon est attendu jusqu'au prochain controle periodique existant ou a une vraie transition centrale. Les inscriptions periodiques ne sont pas supprimees.

**Limite de certification :** ni remplacement positif pendant une operation suspendue, ni hook reentrant, double annulation imbriquee, callback deja distribue avant annulation, retrait suspendu ou ON reussi croisant OFF ne sont garantis par A. Ces courses restent possibles ; leur existence n'est pas niee. OFF rencontre sur le chemin du refus reste obligatoire : cette limite ne permet pas de laisser sa continuation envoyer ON ou reposer ses timers.

**Adequation et minimalite :** A traite la reaction fautive et la continuation qui ignore le refus. Propagation seule, traitement du seul tick ou simple garde « deja deleste » ne suffisent pas aux cas relus. La surface de six fonctions et un auxiliaire de la conception est proportionnee a ces deux causes locales ; ce n'est pas une preuve de minimum absolu ni une garantie de moindre risque mesuree. L'option B de durcissement general n'est pas recommandee pour ce lot.

**Reduction proposee par rapport au rapport et a la 0.1 :** reporter le montant energetique selon chauffe appliquee en P1 independant et retirer les garanties universelles de concurrence. P0 doit supprimer les rafales issues du refus, pas garantir l'exactitude physique de chaque credit ni corriger toutes les courses du scheduler. L'abandon propre deja deleste, avec coupure eventuelle et attente d'un controle existant, est un changement visible a approuver explicitement avec les exclusions (D-01).

### 2. Context and Objectives

A refusal is normal when measured power of 7,600 plus startup power of 3,500 exceeds the 11,000 limit. The problem is the reaction to that refusal: it can immediately restart the control that just requested ON, multiply cycles and leave multiple end-of-cycle callbacks. Bursts can then amplify the energy counter and notifications, without evidence of incorrect physical heating.

**Facts reread in local code:**

- `UnderlyingSwitch.turn_on()` forces a state update after every refusal, even when shedding was already active (S-01).
- The first shedding transition cancels the cycle and requests OFF for all underlyings; the `power` preset lowers the target but does not mean HVAC OFF (S-02/S-03).
- State-change flags remain true until heating control returns; setters retain an already true change flag (S-03).
- TPI control can call `start_cycle(force=True)` again; the running-cycle guard only retains the non-forced path. Timers are installed after awaiting `turn_on()` (S-04/S-05).
- A cycle end calls `incremente_energy()` and restarts the cycle; the formula uses the algorithm's raw duty ratio (S-05/S-06).

**Falsifiable local hypothesis H-01:** forced recalculation after refusal sustains unnecessary reentrancy; nested calls resuming after cancellations can install multiple timers, some losing their cancellation references. Inspection supports this mechanism, but neither reproduction nor the number of surviving timers is established. Even one nested restart may suffice: suppressing only repetitions after the first refusal does not prove a fix.

**P0 objective:** address this trigger close to refusal: immediately propagate state without positively restarting heating from refusal, then abandon the refused attempt before any next ON command or rearming. Retain first OFF, reconciliation, stable idempotence and legitimate recovery. Already-shedding cleanup shall also request OFF for relays belonging to the still-current cycle: dropping timers alone could leave a relay ON without scheduled shutdown.

**Proposed A scope:** native TPI `over_switch`, single/multi, virtual/inverted; first real and subsequent refusals; coherent or inconsistent effective state; effective OFF encountered during awaits of an attempt that actually returns refusal; serialized recovery and positive changes. Start with at most one referenced timer set and no prior orphans; measure after bounded processing completes. Zero scheduler timers after abandonment is expected until the next existing periodic control or genuine central transition. Periodic registrations are not removed.

**Certification boundary:** A guarantees neither positive replacement during a suspended operation, reentrant hooks, nested double cancellation, a callback dispatched before cancellation, suspended removal nor successful ON crossing OFF. These races remain possible; their existence is not denied. OFF encountered on the refusal path remains mandatory: this boundary does not allow its continuation to send ON or reinstall timers.

**Suitability and minimality:** A addresses both the faulty reaction and the continuation ignoring refusal. Propagation alone, tick-only handling or an "already shedding" guard cannot satisfy the inspected cases. The design's six functions and one helper are proportionate to these two local causes; this is neither proof of an absolute minimum nor measured lower risk. General-hardening option B is not recommended for this batch.

**Proposed reduction against the report and 0.1:** defer energy amounts based on applied heating to independent P1 and remove universal concurrency guarantees. P0 shall remove refusal-originated bursts, not ensure every credit's physical accuracy or fix every scheduler race. Clean abandonment while already shedding, with possible shutdown and waiting for existing control, is a visible change requiring explicit approval together with exclusions (D-01).

### 6. Functional Constraints

- **Compatibility:** no new configuration, migration, public API, dependency or mandatory plugin adaptation. Restrict no-positive-control propagation to native TPI; preserve other handlers by default. Check shared explicit gate-refusal effects and keep historical no-explicit-result returns and errors distinct from power refusal. Retain legitimate Auto TPI hooks and valve branches.
- **Availability:** no refusal loop or permanent regulation blockage within A scope; retain serialized commands and OFF on real refusal. Recovery may await existing periodic control every `cycle_min` or genuine central exit; no retry, deferred task, global synchronization or general redesign requested. No established maximum physical delay or numerical CPU budget.
- **Functional safety:** retain refusals and protections without disabling shedding or bypassing command gates. Simulated OFF issuance is observable but does not certify physical shutdown. Linky apparent readings and configured power are not electrical certification.
- **User experience:** no HA restart required to leave the corrected scenario; preset and target reflect effective decisions. No claim is made to repair orphaned timers already accumulated before an update in place.
- **Privacy:** use fictitious identifiers in examples; do not copy a complete configuration or Linky identifier. No additional verified regulatory constraint.
- **Known residual risks:** suspended positive replacement, reentrant hooks, double cancellation, already dispatched callback, suspended removal and concurrent successful ON remain unhardened. They can lose a decision or produce late/duplicate effects; A is not a general priority certificate. An error/gate may prevent physical OFF without permitting bypass. These limits require D-01.
- **Current phase:** SPEC + DESIGN authorized in cycle 2; only this Markdown changed here. Review/design preserved; no code, configuration, dependency, environment or HA-state change, no functional test execution or subagent. Design 0.2 exists and informs conditional convergence, not development authorization.

### 8. Out-of-Scope Functions

- **Deferred P1:** energy estimation based on applied heating, interruption prorating and defining actual heating time. Removing bursts is mandatory in P0; correcting the amount per cycle is not.
- General scheduler/cancellation/removal redesign, generation propagated to all callbacks/hooks, global lock, coalescing or new retry: not recommended for A; no established need from refusal alone.
- Latest-positive-decision guarantees during suspended replacement, uniqueness under nested double cancellation/reentrant hooks, neutralization of already dispatched callbacks, suspended removal and successful ON/OFF atomicity: explicitly removed from A certification, not declared nonexistent or harmless. Hardening requires separate scope or B arbitration.
- Automatic repair of timers accumulated before delivery, global reload cleanup and HA restoration changes: no promise without separate reproduction. Cancelling timers created by the P0 scenario remains mandatory.
- Historical-statistics correction, reported restoration jump, `total_energy_unit=W` label, general VA/W conversion, threshold, TPI coefficient or priority changes: distinct causes/scopes or insufficient evidence.
- Generally disabling heating under `power`, the sensor or callbacks: contrary to objectives, not an acceptable fix.
- Implementation not authorized. SPEC + DESIGN authorized in cycle 2; this intervention changes only the specification, neither review nor design.
